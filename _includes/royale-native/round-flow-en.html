One round. Two encounters.

  1. 01
    You attack

    Choose three cards, or a four- / five-card bomb.

  2. 02
    Rival defends

    They see your play before responding.

  3. 03
    Resolve

    Attack wins: deal wounds. Defense wins: earn swaps.

  4. 04
    Switch roles

    Your rival attacks; you defend. No hand refill yet.

  5. 05
    Refill after the round

    Keep unused cards; both hands refill to eight.

A player reaching 5 wounds ends the game immediately.
