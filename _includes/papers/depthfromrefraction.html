基于折射的自标定深度恢复

Zhihu Chen, Kwan-Yee K. Wong, Yasuyuki Matsushita, Xiaolong Zhu, Miaomiao Liu

摘要

本文提出一种利用光的折射获取深度的新方法。使用固定的透视相机拍摄同一场景两次：第一次直接拍摄，第二次在场景与相机之间放置透明介质。随后根据场景点在两幅图像中的位移恢复深度图。与现有的基于折射的深度恢复方法不同，这一方法无需预先知道透明介质的位姿和折射率，而是直接从输入图像中恢复它们，因此称为基于折射的自标定深度恢复。合成数据和真实场景的实验均验证了所提方法的有效性。

Self-Calibrating Depth from Refraction

Zhihu Chen, Kwan-Yee K. Wong, Yasuyuki Matsushita, Xiaolong Zhu and Miaomiao Liu

Abstract

In this paper, we introduce a novel method for depth ac- quisition based on refraction of light. A scene is captured twice by a fixed perspective camera, with the first image captured directly by the camera and the second by placing a transparent medium between the scene and the camera. A depth map of the scene is then recovered from the displace- ments of scene points in the images. Unlike other existing depth from refraction methods, our method does not require the knowledge of the pose and refractive index of the trans- parent medium, but can recover them directly from the in- put images. We hence call our method self-calibrating depth from refraction. Experimental results on both synthetic and real-world data are presented, which demonstrate the effec- tiveness of the proposed method.
