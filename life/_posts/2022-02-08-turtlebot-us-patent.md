US11242219B2, *Story Monitoring Method When Robot Takes Elevator, Electronic Device, and Computer Storage Medium*, was granted on February 8, 2022. I am a co-inventor.

The patent relates to floor monitoring during elevator travel and belongs to the same patent family as the Chinese grant CN107344688B. It connects with my earlier work on autonomous elevator use and navigation across floors with TurtleBot.
