Co-authored *Neural MMO 2.0: A Massively Multi-task Addition to Massively Multi-agent Learning*, published in the NeurIPS 2023 Datasets and Benchmarks Track.

The open research environment combines large populations of agents with a flexible task system, supporting evaluation on tasks, maps and opponents not encountered during training. This publication complements our work co-organizing the Neural MMO challenges.

[NeurIPS paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/hash/9ca22870ae0ba55ee50ce3e2d269e5de-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.html) · [Project](https://neuralmmo.github.io/) · [Competitions and research](/projects/#neural-mmo-challenges)

The paper appears in the [conference poster track](https://neurips.cc/virtual/2023/poster/73652) at NeurIPS in December 2023. [Official meeting dates](https://neurips.cc/Conferences/2023/Dates).
