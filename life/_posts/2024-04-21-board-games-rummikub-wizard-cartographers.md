Played [Rummikub](https://rummikub.com/), [Wizard](https://blog.amigo-spiele.de/wizard-reihe/), [Cartographers](https://thunderworksgames.com/products/cartographers-board-game) and [Modern Art](https://www.cmon.com/products/modern-art/).

Rummikub stood out for the many ways to rearrange combinations. Wizard made a good shorter game between longer sessions: flexible rounds, frequent feedback and plenty of laughs. Modern Art remained a favorite for how much strategy and conversation emerge from such simple rules.
