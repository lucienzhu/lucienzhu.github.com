CN107344688B, covering a method and device for monitoring floors while a robot rides an elevator, was granted in China on May 14, 2019. I am a co-inventor.

The initial Chinese application was filed in May 2016, following earlier TurtleBot prototyping. The grant date is distinct from the prototype development period.
