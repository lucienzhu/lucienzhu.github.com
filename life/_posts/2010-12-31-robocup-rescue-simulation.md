Participated in the 2010 RoboCup China competition and received a third prize in the Rescue Simulation category, an early experience with multi-agent systems.

The [RoboCup Rescue Simulation](https://rescuesim.robocup.org/research/) setting studies how agents coordinate disaster response, including firefighting, clearing blocked roads and rescuing civilians. It brings together task allocation, limited communication and decisions under incomplete information.
