Presented “Learning Go from Scratch” at the second Tencent AI Lab Academic Forum in March 2018, sharing the reinforcement learning training process behind FineArt and the team’s technical explorations.

The original abstract described FineArt’s progression from learning from human game records to reinforcement learning from scratch, and how training AI prompted the team to revisit its understanding of Go. At the time, my work included algorithm research and training for the FineArt project.

This summary is based on the original speaker introduction and talk abstract. The slides and recording have not been recovered. The original forum URL now serves Tencent Yuanbao rather than the event page.
