Started the year playing [Modern Art](https://www.cmon.com/products/modern-art/), [Carcassonne](https://www.hans-im-glueck.de/en/game/carcassonne/) and [Citadels](https://www.zmangames.com/game/citadels/).

Modern Art was especially absorbing: simple rules, plenty to calculate, and a lot riding on each bid. We kept talking through the decisions afterward. Citadels also made a good impression with its mix of character selection, deduction and collecting districts.
