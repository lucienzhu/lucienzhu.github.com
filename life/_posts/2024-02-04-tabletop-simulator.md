Tried [Tabletop Simulator](https://www.tabletopsimulator.com/). It provides a scene in which players can move pieces and manipulate components such as dice, but I found the interaction less intuitive than I expected.

That left me thinking about the interface of digital board games: how do we preserve the ease of reaching for a piece, moving it and seeing what happened?
