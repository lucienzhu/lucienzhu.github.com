Ran early playtests of the card game that became [3Card Royale](/projects/#3card-royale). Tried different ways to handle damage, three-card attack and defense, and progression between games.

The feedback was concrete: some versions were too slow, certain hands appeared too rarely, and swapping the last two cards did not feel like a meaningful decision. These sessions helped shape the next versions.

[Play the current version](https://lucienzhu.github.io/3card-royale/?lang=en).
