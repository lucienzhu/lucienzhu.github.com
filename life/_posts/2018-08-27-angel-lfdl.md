On August 27, 2018, the Linux Foundation announced that Angel, contributed by Tencent, had joined the LF Deep Learning Foundation. Angel is a distributed machine learning platform built around parameter servers.

The announcement quoted me as a Tencent senior AI researcher and LFDL Technical Advisory Council member, discussing open-source collaboration and making deep learning easier to use.
