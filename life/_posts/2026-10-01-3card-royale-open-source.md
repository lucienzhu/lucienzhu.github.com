Designed 3Card Royale, a two-player game played with a standard deck, combining attack, defense and a race from 2 to Ace. The bilingual browser playtest lets players try it against a computer opponent.

Open-sourced the rules and portable game implementations in JavaScript, Python, Rust and C++, with a TypeScript API and a Gymnasium environment for reinforcement-learning experiments.
