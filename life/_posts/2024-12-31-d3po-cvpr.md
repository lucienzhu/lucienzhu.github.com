Co-authored *Using Human Feedback to Fine-tune Diffusion Models without Any Reward Model*, accepted at CVPR 2024.

D3PO adapts direct preference optimization to diffusion policies, allowing human preferences to guide image generation without training a separate reward model. The first preprint appeared in November 2023; the conference publication is recorded here under 2024.
