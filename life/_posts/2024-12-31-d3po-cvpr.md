Co-authored *Using Human Feedback to Fine-tune Diffusion Models without Any Reward Model*, accepted at CVPR 2024.

D3PO adapts direct preference optimization to diffusion policies, allowing human preferences to guide image generation without training a separate reward model. The first preprint appeared in November 2023; the paper was presented at CVPR in June 2024.

[CVPR paper](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Yang_Using_Human_Feedback_to_Fine-tune_Diffusion_Models_without_Any_Reward_CVPR_2024_paper.html) · [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.13231) · [Code](https://github.com/yk7333/D3PO)

The [official conference program](https://media.eventhosts.cc/Conferences/CVPR2024/CVPR_main_conf_2024.pdf) lists the paper in Poster Session 2 in June 2024, poster 402.
