Developed a DnD-inspired role-playing prototype that runs on Apple Vision Pro, bringing together AI and spatial computing. The project explored how AI NPC interactions and virtual tabletop adventures could fit into a physical room.

Early XR exploration began in 2023 with a Quest Pro approach using spatial anchors. Design work covered NPCs moving between real and virtual spaces and a lobby–map–dungeon structure for organizing adventures. The project subsequently reached a runnable Vision Pro prototype.
