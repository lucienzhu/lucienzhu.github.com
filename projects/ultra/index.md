Worked on Ultra, a multiplayer game prototype where humans and AI share a real-time world of exploration, gathering, survival and combat. Different AI abilities and play styles create opportunities for both cooperation and competition with human players.

The team also organized an AI esports event around the prototype, bringing trained AI and human players into the same competition and presenting their strategies through spectator views.
