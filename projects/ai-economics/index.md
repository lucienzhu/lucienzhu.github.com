Worked on an independent, runnable economic simulation prototype with resource exchange between agents. The project explored how individual needs and differences in production ability can connect through trade, providing an environment for studying division of labor and collective economic behavior.

Environment design covered resource gathering, tool production, limited inventories and local exchanges. These mechanisms framed the research questions around individual incentives, supply and demand, and interactions across a population of agents.
