Worked on AI Jianghu, a wuxia text-adventure prototype where players choose a character and story opening, type their own actions, and let AI continue the narrative. The project explored language generation as a game mechanic, with players shaping the story through repeated interaction.

A playable demonstration was shown at ChinaJoy 2021. The archived video shows character-driven stories, open text input and AI-generated continuations.
