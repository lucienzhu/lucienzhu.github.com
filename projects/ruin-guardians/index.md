Worked on Ruin Guardians (遗迹守护者), an asymmetric game prototype that brought game design and multi-agent reinforcement learning together. The two sides have different numbers, abilities and objectives: a team uses keys and ladders to reach treasure chests, while a defender pursues them and tries to stop the treasure hunt.

The project explored how level layouts, item mechanics and team objectives could create opportunities for learned cooperation and competition. Game AI development included training separate policies for the opposing sides, curriculum learning and self-play, with playable builds for human–AI interaction.

The video below is a complete, silent 63.6-second recording from March 8, 2021. It shows an early development build with blue treasure hunters and a yellow defender. A later playable version was demonstrated at ChinaJoy 2021.
